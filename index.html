Capacitor bank

Measure every capacitor with a digital multimeter. You may need to disconnect a wire from the connection to the coil so the meter reads that capacitor alone. Type each reading in the box for that switch. The values are stored in this browser, so they are still here next time you open the page. Do this quarterly. Capacitance fades with usage, and every switch depends on these values.

Each switch is a summary of all the switches that follow it. The microfarads on a switch should come out about the same as the sum of every switch after it. Where that step is about 2, one switch on is worth every switch below it.

Type your measured microfarads. They are stored in this browser.

Measure the coil

Turn on one known capacitor, such as the 4 µF on switch E. Set the frequency near 840 Hz for that switch, bring the output up to about 0.5 V, and move the frequency until the voltage peaks. Fine-tune by tenths of a hertz. Enter that capacitance and the frequency where the voltage was highest.
